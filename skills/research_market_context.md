# Research Market Context

When gathering data for the Market Context section of a PRD or Spec, follow these guidelines:

## 1. Industry Reports & Statistics
Scenario: You need macro-level data on the market size and growth.
Action: Search for recent reports (e.g., Gartner, Forrester, Statista) related to the industry. Look for Total Addressable Market (TAM) and Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR).

## 2. Trend Analysis
Scenario: The market is shifting due to new technology or regulations.
Action: Identify key trends driving adoption or churn in the specific product category.

## 3. Demographics and Segmentation
Scenario: Understanding the primary segments of the market.
Action: Break down the market into serviceable addressable market (SAM) and identify the specific niches the product aims to capture.
