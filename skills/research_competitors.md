# Research Competitors

When analyzing competitors for a product plan, follow these guidelines:

## 1. Feature Parity Analysis
Scenario: You need to know what features are table stakes.
Action: Compare the proposed feature set against direct competitors. Note where the competitor excels and where they fall short.

## 2. Pricing Models
Scenario: Understanding market expectations for cost.
Action: Analyze how competitors price similar features (e.g., flat rate, usage-based, tiered). 

## 3. Customer Reviews & Sentiment
Scenario: Finding competitor weaknesses.
Action: Look at G2, Capterra, or Trustpilot reviews for competitors. Focus on 2-star and 3-star reviews to identify specific pain points and unmet needs that your product can address.
