# Research User Personas

When defining User Personas for a PRD or Spec, follow these guidelines:

## 1. User Interviews & Feedback
Scenario: You have existing qualitative data from users.
Action: Synthesize quotes, pain points, and behavioral patterns from past user interviews, support tickets, and sales calls.

## 2. Demographic and Firmographic Data
Scenario: Defining who the user is.
Action: Identify the user's role, company size, goals, and technical proficiency. Distinguish between the "Buyer" and the "End User" if applicable.

## 3. Empathy Mapping
Scenario: Mapping the user's daily life and interactions with the product.
Action: Document what the user thinks, feels, says, and does when encountering the problem your product solves.
